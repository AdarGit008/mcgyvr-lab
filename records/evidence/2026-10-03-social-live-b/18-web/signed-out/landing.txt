 How it works 
 Add your rig. Run one command on the machine with the GPUs. Its cards join the pool while it is online. 
 The hub runs the models. It splits each model across cards from many people's rigs. 
 You get an endpoint and a key. Plug them into any tool that works with the OpenAI API. You use the pool from your own tools; there is no chat on this site. 
 Give to take. You can use other people's cards only while one of your own rigs is online and lending.

 Four ways to share 
 You pick one for each rig, and can change it any time. 
 General pool (the default) 
 Your cards join everyone's. The hub runs models across them, and you use the pool through your endpoint. Your requests run on other members' rigs, and the owner of the rig that runs the start of the model can read your prompts. 
 Hitchhike 
 You run your own model on your own rig and let others ride in its spare slots; your requests go first. You can ride on other people's models too, if you turn it on. Whoever runs the model can read the prompts that ride on it. 
 Crew 
 Lend your cards to a group you trust. People join a crew with its invite code, and the crew's owner picks the models it runs. 
 Solo 
 Your rig runs only for you, outside the hub's sharing: it neither lends nor borrows. You can still share your setup with others.
